Eco Tips & Insights

Simple habits for a sustainable lifestyle.

🚲

Choose Greener Travel

Walk, cycle or use public transport whenever practical to reduce emissions.

💡

Save Electricity

Switch off lights and fans when they are not in use.

💧

Save Water

Turn off taps when brushing and avoid unnecessary water wastage.

♻️

Reduce Plastic

Carry reusable bags and bottles instead of single-use plastic.

🌳

Plant Trees

Grow plants and support local green spaces and biodiversity.

🥗

Avoid Food Waste

Plan meals and use food wisely to reduce waste.
