Eco-Friendly Tips 💚

Simple habits for a healthier and greener planet.

🚲

Choose Green Travel

Walk, cycle or use public transport to reduce vehicle emissions.

💡

Save Electricity

Switch off lights and appliances when they are not in use.

🛍️

Reduce Plastic

Carry reusable bags and bottles instead of single-use plastic.

🌳

Plant More Trees

Grow plants and protect trees to support the environment.

💧

Save Water

Turn off taps properly and avoid unnecessary water wastage.

♻️

Recycle Waste

Separate dry and wet waste and recycle reusable materials.
